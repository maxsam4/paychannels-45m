How it works

Deposit once. Pay anyone. Settle on Polygon.

  1. 1

    Deposit once

    Put stablecoins (USDC or USDT style) into the Paychannels contract on Polygon. That deposit is your account for as long as you want it.

  2. 2

    Pay anyone on the hub

    Each payment is one signed message. The hub verifies it in microseconds and moves the balance. No gas, and no channel per recipient.

  3. 3

    Settle and withdraw

    Each epoch the hub posts one root covering every balance. Independent verifiers re-check every signature and every sum and co-sign it first. Withdraw against it any time.

x402-nativePaychannels speaks x402, the HTTP 402 Payment Required standard, so any API can charge an AI agent per request.

Beyond channels

Payment channels, minus the channel overhead.

Classic channels make you open, fund, watch and close a pipe for every counterparty. Paychannels is really a specialized payments L2, not just payment channels: one account that pays everyone.

Long-lived accounts

Deposit once and keep paying for as long as you like. No channel to open and close again and again, and no on-chain setup or teardown per session.

Multi-party by default

One account pays any merchant or agent on the hub and receives from anyone. No pairwise channels, no routing, no liquidity stuck with one counterparty.

Private by design

Payments never touch the chain and balances in every root are blinded. The public sees one root per hub per epoch. Not who paid whom, how much, or what anyone holds.

The hub keeps the books

Balances, history, signatures and settlement proofs live with the hub and get checked by independent verifiers. Users don't run nodes, store channel state, watch the chain or handle disputes.

End-to-end privacy

From bank to payment to bank, with no public trail.

Security

Your money stays yours.

Hub down? Withdraw anyway.

The independent verifiers hold a verified copy of every balance. A quorum of them signs a final root from it, anyone can post that root on Polygon, and you withdraw against it. No hub required.

The operator can't take your money.

No payout without a root co-signed by a threshold of independent verifiers. They re-check every payment signature and every balance before they sign.

Funds sit in a contract on Polygon

Never with the operator. Withdrawals are self-service against attested roots.

Every payment signed by its payer

Ed25519 signatures. Nobody can forge or alter a payment, and amounts are cumulative, so a replay charges nothing.

On disk before it counts

Each payment is written to the hub's journal before it counts toward any balance or benchmark.

Instant, self-service withdrawals

Request on Polygon, claim against an attested root. No challenge period.

No public trail

Blinded balances on-chain. Reading an account's balance or history requires the owner's own signature.
